# Folklore 120: Hollgrot, the Outcast

Hollgrot was born to a nomadic orc clan. At ten orc years, tradition cast him
out to survive alone and prove the strength of his blood.

He chose Nibiru's barren cold, built a lodge, hunted, and discovered that
solitude could become skill rather than sentence. There he found people who
called themselves Nibirans and accepted them as family despite the old law of
his pilgrimage.

His later theft of Tophian totems stained that belonging and forced costly
restitution. Hollgrot's tale therefore preserves both truths: an outcast can
find a home, and finding one does not free him from what he chooses afterward.
