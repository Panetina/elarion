# Folklore 130: Corrin, Iceborn

Corrin followed a trail of blood behind his family home and found only his
mother's scarf. A blinding flash took consciousness and memory alike.

He woke in Nibiru knowing that something important had been erased. Distrust
became instinct, while the need for community remained just as deep. One dream
returned: blood, scarf, house.

Corrin vowed to build a place where contempt and fear were unnecessary, even
as he struggled to offer others the trust such a place required. To recover
the lost house was to recover himself, whatever waited there.
