# Folklore 138: The Voice of Restraint

Among Toph's early debates, one citizen repeatedly argued for term limits,
earned authority, forgiveness, and retaliation only when attacked.

During the totem dispute, the Voice of Restraint urged the territory to avoid
turning a small offense into permanent hostility. Strength, the fragments
suggest, included knowing when not to escalate.

No roleplay name was fixed in the archive. The epithet preserves the position
that shaped the record.
