# Folklore 149: Nify of Toph

Nify was among Toph's most persistent recorded citizens.

The surviving material places Nify within the territory's planning, building,
and public conversation, but offers no accepted account of earlier life. The
archive remembers participation rather than inventing ancestry.

Toph's walls were raised by more hands than its official histories named.
Nify's volume restores one of them.
