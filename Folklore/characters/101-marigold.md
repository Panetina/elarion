# Folklore 101: Marigold, the Cursed Castaway

Marigold once called the sea freedom, battlefield, and home. A ship without a
natural silhouette found her crew in fog and swallowed vessel and companions
alike. The tide returned Marigold alone upon Nibiru's coast, green-skinned,
bright-eyed, and burdened with a hunger that death should have ended.

She carved a pirate refuge into the cliffs and waited for sailors who might
know the fog-ship. Instead she found Triccy, a fisherbear who led her from the
coast into Nibiru's hidden snow. Together they began searching the fractured
land for the cause of Marigold's curse and the truth of the sea that refused
to keep her.
