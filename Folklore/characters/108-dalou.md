# Folklore 108: Dalou, the Hidden Twin

Dalou and her pale-haired twin Yene were born to forbidden blood and raised in
secret. When disguised agents found the family, captivity replaced childhood.
Their mother bought escape with her life and one command: hide who you are and
live.

Dalou lost Yene's hand in the corridors. She escaped alone and carried the
failure through years of wandering, sword practice, concealed magic, and
fading hope.

Mount Hua finally offered a place where potions and enchantments were practiced
without shame. Dalou stopped running there, though she never stopped wondering
whether her father or sister still walked some distant road.
