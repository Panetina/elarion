# Folklore 109: Sappy, the Exiled Scribe

Sappy rose from peasant birth to the lower halls of Mount Hua's scholars. While
translating writings older than the Sundering, he found repeated references to
hidden Wilderness enclaves and one command: *Rend the Skies*.

His superiors seized the texts, denied the discovery, erased his standing, and
offered exile in place of death. Sappy fled through Emeraldis into Solaris.

Among the sands he became a builder of markets and a seeker of the truth that
had cost him his home. The phrase followed him into sleep. He never learned
whether it described a warning, a place, or an instruction already carried
out.
