# Folklore 122: Magpie, the Wingless Sprite

Magpie emerged from the shifting Wilderness with no offered past and two scars
where wings may once have grown.

Flowers, tame creatures, and shining objects delighted her without regard for
their market value. Others named her after the collecting bird. She accepted
the name and shared her finds generously with anyone who earned her quick
friendship.

Magpie settled in Emeraldis to build a garden where every beautiful fragment
could be displayed. Whatever had taken her wings did not take her instinct to
gather joy and make it visible.
