# Folklore 155: Lemon of Solaris

Lemon's name appears along the Solarian roads while the territory rebuilt
trade, homes, and public identity.

No earlier life was recorded. The surviving pattern proves participation but
not profession, lineage, or secret. The archive leaves those spaces empty.

Solaris was more than its famous merchants. Lemon's volume preserves one of
the citizens who made the desert inhabited between the caravans.
