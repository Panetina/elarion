# Folklore 126: Elandor of Lost Valemont

Elandor had once belonged to House Valemont of the golden Emeraldian lands.
His father practiced diplomacy in Harmonium, teaching that peace was forged
through words before the world burned home, house, and family away.

Education, redstone knowledge, a traveler's sack, and a few emeralds survived.
Elandor crossed all Five as merchant and diplomat, learning customs while
searching for the force that destroyed Emeraldia.

He abandoned the house's rank but not its duty. His vow remained: let no path
go untrodden and no history unspoken, because a name survives while one person
remembers it.
