# Folklore 136: The Stone Magistrate

The Stone Magistrate crossed between Emeraldis and Toph in surviving
correspondence, discussing monasteries, farms, merit, government, and judgment.

The Magistrate argued that leadership should emerge from proven conduct rather
than haste and believed a judge's duty suited a character more than a ruler's
seat. During conflict, the same voice urged institutions before vengeance.

No accepted roleplay name survives. The title records the civic function the
fragments reveal.
