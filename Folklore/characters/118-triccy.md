# Folklore 118: Triccy, the Fisherbear

Triccy was born under a full moon to a revered fisher and protector of ancestral
waters. She learned angling, patience, and the difficult balance between wild
strength and civilized grace.

The Sundering took her family and home. In Nibiru she wandered until curiosity
overcame solitude. Fish feasts, bright stories, and unexpected friendship
became her way of rebuilding connection.

On the coast she found Marigold, a cursed castaway, and led her inland toward
the snow. Thus a fisherbear seeking belonging became the first companion in
another exile's search for truth.
