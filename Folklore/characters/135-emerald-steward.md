# Folklore 135: The Emerald Steward

The personal name of Emeraldis's first steward is absent from the surviving
roleplay register.

Territory records show a late arrival who explored the forests, helped choose
the settlement, encouraged its citizens, and eventually spoke for Emeraldis
among the Five. The Steward treated leadership less as a crown than as the
work of finding where people could safely begin.

The archive preserves the office as an epithet rather than attaching the wrong
name to the deeds.
