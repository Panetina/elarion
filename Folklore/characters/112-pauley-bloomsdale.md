# Folklore 112: Pauley Bloomsdale

Pauley was born to an Emeraldis family whose nature-bound name survived longer
than the family itself. After their deaths she left the green, withholding her
surname as if distance could separate grief from identity.

Fate carried her into Nibiru's cold. It was an absurd home for someone raised
among lush land, yet she adapted and helped others recover the security she
had lost.

Nibiru later chose Pauley as a leader. Beneath the office remained the same
purpose: take hold of uncertain fate, make a plan, and leave fewer people
unprotected than the world had left her.
