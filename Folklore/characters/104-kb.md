# Folklore 104: Kb, the Architect

Kb designed walls with pride and then became trapped inside their meanings.
Politics moved outside while the architect listened for whispers, cackles, and
warnings scratched where no warning should have been.

Others called the rambling distraction. Yet Nibiru's cold damaged fewer
structures wherever Kb worked. Roofs held. Supports endured. Rooms remained
habitable while their maker stared too long at the writing on the wall.

The old saying attached to Kb is preserved without explanation: every building
made with pride keeps some part of its architect inside.
