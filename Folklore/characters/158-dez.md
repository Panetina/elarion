# Folklore 158: Dez of Solaris

Dez appears briefly but repeatedly upon Solaris's old arrival record.

The scrolls preserve no origin and assign no public role. They only prove that
Dez crossed the sand, entered the settlement, and belonged to the final season
before silence.

Not every recovered name is a legend. The archive keeps them because a
civilization is diminished when only legends are allowed to have existed.
