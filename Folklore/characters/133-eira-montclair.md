# Folklore 133: Eira Montclair

Eira was named for flawless snow and inherited its silence.

She withdrew from mortal quarrels to ask why the world abandoned its children,
why suffering repeated, and whether fate was merely cruelty given ceremony.
Among Mount Hua's cold peaks she built a sanctuary of ink, frost, ritual, and
research.

Stars appeared to her as broken pieces of one concealed truth. Eira resolved
to assemble them, however long the mountains kept their answer.
