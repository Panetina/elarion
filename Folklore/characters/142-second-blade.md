# Folklore 142: The Second Blade

The Second Blade appears in the assassination affair beside the named killer
and the broker who set events in motion.

Accounts disagree on participation, knowledge, and responsibility. Some place
the figure near the contract; others treat the association itself as the only
proven fact. No final judgment survives.

The archive refuses to supply certainty that the witnesses did not possess.
The epithet records presence without inventing innocence or guilt.
