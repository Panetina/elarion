# Folklore 103: Num, Keeper of Living Stories

Num gathered stories until the library could keep them without constant hands.
Then the keeper turned toward flowers.

To Num, this was not an abandonment of memory. Roots held lineage. Blooming
marked arrival. Withering recorded departure. Each bouquet was a sentence
written in color and season, following lessons first taught by a grandmother
who listened to soil.

Num remained in Emeraldis and built a florist's life among the green. Those who
expected an old archivist found a gardener instead. Those who listened longer
understood that the archive had merely learned to grow.
