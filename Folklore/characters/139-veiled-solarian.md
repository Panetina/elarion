# Folklore 139: The Veiled Solarian

The Veiled Solarian proposed emblems, guarded plans, and spoke of nefarious
lore with enough humor that later archivists could not decide where the joke
ended.

The fragments show no desire for an early war, only a strong concern that
Solaris should possess private intentions as well as public trade. Deleted
messages became part of the reputation.

With no roleplay name preserved, the archive records a citizen who understood
that secrecy itself can become a political instrument.
