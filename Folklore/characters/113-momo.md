# Folklore 113: Momo, Who Gave His Sword to the Sun

Momo had marched in a glorious army before the world ended. Under Solaris'
heat, the dead became indistinguishable from the sand.

He offered his sword to the sun and refused the life of a warrior. What came
after was deliberately unwritten. The blank space was not forgetfulness, but a
choice: his next identity would be lived rather than inherited from war.

Old storytellers used Momo's fragment when speaking to soldiers who believed a
weapon was the only proof they had existed.
