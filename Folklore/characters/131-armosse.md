# Folklore 131: Armosse, the Fallen Druid

Armosse spent nearly all his power defending distant forests in a brutal war.
The wild voice that remained came only as a whisper, accompanied by memory of
a friend he could not save.

In Emeraldis he chose farming, discipline, and quiet bonds. Labor rebuilt body
and spirit without pretending the old power had returned. He avoided conflict,
spoke according to principle, and kept shield and axe ready.

Armosse no longer sought mastery over the wild. He sought enough strength to
ensure that home and friends would not be lost a second time.
