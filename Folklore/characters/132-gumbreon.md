# Folklore 132: Gumbreon, the Cornerstone

Gumbreon survived the Sundering as a warrior who knew when to stop swinging.
In Nibiru he exchanged weapon for pickaxe and learned the veins beneath the
ice.

His maps were trusted, his judgment sought, and his stone used for roads and
walls designed to outlast weather and panic. He did not lead, yet people
listened.

Gumbreon built no monument to himself. His legacy was foundation: the hidden
work upon which every visible structure depended.
