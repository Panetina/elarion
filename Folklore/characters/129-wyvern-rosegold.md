# Folklore 129: Wyvern Rosegold

Wyvern Rosegold was born to Tophian builders and orphaned when an attack took
their lives. Years without a home made architecture more than craft.

He gathered resources beside Callista Terrafall and shared her determination
to turn hardship into a kingdom. His stated ambition was unrestrained: build
the finest structure the world had ever seen.

For Wyvern, greatness was not ornament. It was the promise that someone else
would not have to grow up without shelter.
