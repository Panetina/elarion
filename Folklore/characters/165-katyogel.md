# Folklore 165: Katyogel, the Owl of Winter

Katyogel descended from Nibiru's pines with wings white as drifting night and
eyes said to be older than fire.

The winter owl chose Isdraugr rather than being tamed by him. Where the
Frostforged searched ruins, Katyogel watched. Travelers claimed the hammer
tested strength while the owl's gaze tested truth.

Old songs called Katyogel a spirit of the first winter and a guide for the
forgotten. Whether spirit or creature, the owl carried what Isdraugr's forge
could not make: memory with wings.
