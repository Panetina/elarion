# Folklore 134: Fixer, the Wayfaring Broker

Fixer appeared at the edges of disputes, missing scrolls, quiet errands, and
problems respectable people wanted solved without witnesses.

No lineage or homeland followed the broker into Mount Hua. Only usefulness and
a price did. Fixer could provide paths, information, and hands willing to do
what the buyer would not.

That talent eventually placed blood upon a contract and brought Toph's demand
for judgment. Fixer survived the settlement, but the agreement narrowed the
broker's reach. From then on, every whispered task carried the memory of the
one that became public.
