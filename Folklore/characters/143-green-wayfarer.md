# Folklore 143: The Green Wayfarer

The Green Wayfarer appears throughout Emeraldis's active days but left no
submitted biography and no stable roleplay name.

Frequent arrivals and departures suggest a citizen woven into the ordinary
rhythm of the territory rather than one remembered for office or scandal. Such
lives are easily erased when history keeps only rulers and wars.

This volume exists to preserve a simpler truth: Emeraldis was inhabited by
people whose daily presence made it a home.
