# Folklore 156: Broccoli of Solaris

Broccoli arrived in Solaris often enough to become part of its active record,
though no character chronicle was submitted.

Later fragments remember the name more clearly than the deeds. That imbalance
is common in the recovered archive: a voice remains recognizable after the
context around it has vanished.

This volume gives Broccoli no invented office. It records a Solarian citizen
whose presence outlasted explanation.
