# Folklore 153: Elysina of Mount Hua

Elysina repeatedly crossed Mount Hua's surviving travel record, yet no account
of origin, profession, or ambition reached the archive.

The evidence establishes a citizen who returned often enough to know the
mountain paths and to be known upon them. That ordinary familiarity is all the
Green Silence allowed to remain.

The archive preserves Elysina's name without manufacturing the missing life.
