# Folklore 157: Kaoz of Emeraldis

Kaoz was deeply present in Emeraldis's surviving correspondence and also
crossed into the world itself.

No personal lore survives. The archive can only place Kaoz among those who
debated, planned, and socialized while the green territory became a society
rather than a collection of shelters.

The chosen name endured. The story around it must remain honestly incomplete.
