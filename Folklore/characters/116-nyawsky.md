# Folklore 116: Nyawsky, the Frog Who Wanted Everything

Nyawsky came from an unnamed swamp where every frog appeared equally small,
green, and forgettable. He alone imagined silk, fine drink, and piles of
shining wealth.

With a patched satchel and confidence larger than experience, he left the reeds
for Solaris. Whether he became a clever merchant, shameless swindler, or merely
a frog with expensive dreams was never settled.

The fragment survives because ambition does not require dignity before it
begins.
