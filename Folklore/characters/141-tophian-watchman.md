# Folklore 141: The Tophian Watchman

The Tophian Watchman appeared irregularly and returned to find cities changed,
offices reassigned, and disputes already in motion.

The fragments describe a citizen willing to police disruption, threaten
probation for reckless officers, and demand summaries after absence. The tone
was blunt, but the concern was civic: who was trusted with force, and who would
stop them when they failed?

No roleplay name survives. The watch remains in the record.
