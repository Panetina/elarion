# Folklore 107: Snowman, the Eternal Champion

Snowman was once a soldier in a war no one could win. He survived not through
prophecy or divine favor, but through a refusal to remain defeated.

Legends multiplied around him: monsters outlasted, thrones broken, titans
felled, and gods taught to fear a mortal who continued rising. Yet the man
beneath the title remembered every failure and every life lost in his shadow.

That memory was the source of his danger. Gods expected other gods to seek
glory. They did not understand a wounded human who had chosen war and would not
permit heaven to end it for him.
