# Folklore 125: Luna Davies

Luna Davies remembered little of the life before the coast. A shipwreck carried
Luna from another continent and left only enough timber for a base camp.

For a month Luna searched without finding people. Mount Hua eventually
answered that solitude with terraces, texts, and citizens who needed a voice.
The castaway became one of the territory's representatives.

The fragment offers no recovered childhood and no explanation for the voyage.
Luna's known life began with wreckage and was built from what remained.
