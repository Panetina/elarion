# Folklore 009: The Gate Kept by the Realm Master

At Harmonium stood a gate to fire.

The Realm Master guarded its rhythm. At appointed times the Nether breach
opened for forty-eight hours, and settlers crossed for materials, danger, and
answers. When the interval ended, the gate closed again. No private ambition
could command it.

Expeditions returned with blaze rods, scorched armor, rare stone, and stories
that improved with every telling. Some accused the keeper of hoarding power.
Others understood that an always-open wound would eventually bleed into the
city.

The gate taught Elarion that access could be a civic instrument. A portal was
not only a doorway; it was a schedule, a risk, a shared cost, and a promise
that someone would still be guarding the return.
