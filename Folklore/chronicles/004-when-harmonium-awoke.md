# Folklore 004: When Harmonium Awoke

Harmonium had stood empty at the center of the Five. Then the territories
returned.

South of the portal, hands raised a courthouse. To the north came a council
hall. Scholars and gardeners planned a library. Traders measured market plots,
roads, docks, and posts. The center was not claimed by one territory; it was
made useful by all of them, which proved more difficult and more important.

The courthouse was completed first. The market followed in arguments,
measurements, and promises. The library became a shared labor between mountain
scholars and the people of the green. For a brief season, the silent heart
held footsteps again.

Modern Elarion calls the restored capital **Worldheart**. Old fragments still
call the place Harmonium. Whether the names mark one city or two ages of the
same city, the purpose remains unchanged: the Realms meet where no Realm rules.
