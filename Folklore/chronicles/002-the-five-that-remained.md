# Folklore 002: The Five That Remained

Only a fraction of the old world remained safely known. Five territories rose
from that fraction.

Mount Hua kept its terraces, sky-islands, scholars, and red petals. Solaris
claimed the burning sands and rebuilt roads through trade. Toph raised stone,
law, and stubborn walls. Nibiru preserved fire beneath ice and remembered
every road that winter tried to erase. Emeraldis listened to roots, rivers, and
the walking Colossi of the green.

Between them stood Harmonium, the empty heart: unclaimed halls, silent markets,
and gates that waited for hands to use them again. Beyond all six places lay
the Wilderness, where ruins had no witnesses and the land no longer obeyed old
maps.

The Five did not begin as one people. They became neighbors because solitude
was more dangerous than disagreement.
