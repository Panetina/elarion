# Folklore 016: Fire Kept, Roads Remembered

Nibiru survived by refusing to forget practical things.

Where was the next shelter? Which ice would hold? Who had fuel? Which tunnel
returned home? Its hunters, miners, builders, fishers, and smiths made memory a
tool against winter. Fire was rationed, roads marked, and those who could
endure were expected to help others endure.

The Frostforged became the territory's clearest symbol: a smith who continued
after death because the work remained unfinished.

Nibiru's journal closes with no triumph. It records a duty: keep the fire,
remember the road, and leave both for the traveler behind you.
