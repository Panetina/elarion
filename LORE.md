# Elarion Lore

Last reviewed: 2026-06-11

Author: Panyel  
Team: Panetina Team

This file records established Elarion canon. Add lore gradually as gameplay
systems become concrete. Future concepts remain in `PLANS.md` until adopted as
canon.

## Elarion

Elarion is the shared civilization formed by its many Realms. Each Realm keeps
its own identity, territory, leadership, customs, and history while
participating in institutions recognized across Elarion.

The present age is known as:

**Elarion: The Verdant Reawakening**

Civilization has returned to a beautiful world already reclaimed by forests,
roots, rivers, and weather. Only scattered buildings, foundations, roads,
graves, sealed records, and monuments survive from the earlier World of
Elarion.

## Worldheart

Worldheart is Elarion's capital and neutral convergence point. It is separate
from the Realm worlds and serves as common ground for shared institutions,
trade, diplomacy, public memory, and the Ancient Portal Gates.

Recovered records call an earlier shared center **Harmonium**. Modern
archivists identify its surviving site with Worldheart. Harmonium is therefore
the historical name used for the old center; Worldheart is the canonical
present-day name.

## The Lost First Age

The recovered first age is divided into:

1. The Age Before.
2. The Sundering.
3. The Fivefold Settlement.
4. The Harmonium Season.
5. The Green Silence.
6. The Verdant Reawakening.

The Sundering broke the known world and left five major surviving territories:
Mount Hua, Solaris, Toph, Nibiru, and Emeraldis. Most land beyond them became
the Wilderness. The survivors rebuilt homes, trade, government, archives, and
Harmonium before the record ended.

The Green Silence describes the unknown interval in which those settlements
vanished and nature reclaimed them. Its cause is deliberately unresolved.

## Recovered Folklore

The numbered source catalog under `Folklore/` preserves histories and
character accounts recovered from the lost first age.

- Historical volumes may contain disputed, incomplete, or contradictory
  accounts.
- Character volumes preserve only that character's story.
- Roleplay names are used in published lore; source account identities are not.
- Missing identities receive archival epithets rather than invented names.
- The archive distinguishes supported fact, recorded belief, and later legend.

In future gameplay, unique Echo Scrolls may be discovered among ruins and
returned to the Worldheart archive. A returned scroll should become permanent,
immutable public memory. It is read directly from an archive display and is
never handed out as a movable book item.

## The Sigil

The Worldheart Treasury mints Elarion's official currency, known as the
**Sigil**. The plural form is **Sigils**.

Every Sigil bears the **Elarion Seal**, the ancient mark recognized by every
Realm. This makes a Sigil more than a piece of currency: it is an official
guarantee that its value is accepted under Elarion's shared civilization.

The registered item is:

```text
elarion:sigil
```

## The Elarion Seal

The Elarion Seal is a unique heraldic mark representing the unity of the
Realms without erasing their individual identities. It appears on official
Sigils and may later appear on Worldheart institutions, records, treaties, and
other shared civic objects.

The Seal is intentionally an abstract government emblem rather than a literal
heart, crown, or gemstone. Like a historical crest or coat of arms, it
represents authority, recognition, and continuity.

## Canon Boundaries

Established:

- Elarion is the shared civilization.
- Realms are its distinct member societies.
- Worldheart is the capital and neutral convergence point.
- The present age is the Verdant Reawakening.
- The earlier shared center was known as Harmonium.
- The old world passed through the Sundering, Fivefold Settlement, Harmonium
  Season, and Green Silence.
- The five recorded old territories were Mount Hua, Solaris, Toph, Nibiru,
  and Emeraldis.
- Numbered recovered histories and character accounts live under `Folklore/`.
- The Worldheart Treasury mints Sigils.
- Every Sigil bears the Elarion Seal.
- The Elarion Seal represents the unity of the Realms.

Not yet established:

- who founded the Worldheart Treasury
- the age or origin of the Elarion Seal
- how the first Realms recognized the Sigil
- the legal structure governing the Treasury
- whether the Seal has magical properties
- what caused the Sundering
- why the first settlements vanished
- how much of old Harmonium survives beneath Worldheart
- who sealed and dispersed the surviving Echo Scrolls

These details should be developed through future gameplay, history, NPCs,
Chronicles, discoveries, and player-created events rather than fixed
prematurely.
