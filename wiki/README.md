## Status

This wiki is admin-first. It documents the systems that can be configured,
tested, or operated today, and it marks unfinished systems clearly.

Status labels:

- `Implemented`: present in source and usable now.
- `Admin-only`: OP level 4 or server-owner workflow.
- `Manual verification needed`: implemented but needs in-game validation on the current modpack.
- `Future`: planned, not implemented.
- `Shell`: module exists but has little or no gameplay behavior.
